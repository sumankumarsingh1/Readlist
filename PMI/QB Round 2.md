# Advanced Question Bank — Senior Project / Delivery / P&L Roles

---

## How do you handle a project that is going off track?
- I first assess the situation using data—timeline slippage, cost variance, and resource constraints.
- I identify root causes (scope creep, dependency delays, skill gaps, or unclear requirements).
- I re-baseline the project plan with realistic timelines and priorities.
- I communicate transparently with stakeholders and reset expectations.
- I implement corrective actions such as resource reallocation or scope prioritization.
- I continuously monitor progress through defined checkpoints to prevent recurrence.

---

## Describe a time you managed conflicting stakeholder expectations.
- I start by understanding each stakeholder’s priorities and constraints.
- I align discussions around business objectives rather than individual preferences.
- I use data and impact analysis to drive objective decision-making.
- I facilitate structured discussions to arrive at mutually agreed priorities.
- I ensure clear documentation and communication of decisions.
- This approach helps in maintaining trust while ensuring project success.

---

## How do you manage scope creep in projects?
- I establish a strong change management process from the beginning.
- I ensure all requirements are clearly defined and agreed upon.
- Any change request is evaluated for impact on cost, timeline, and resources.
- I involve stakeholders in decision-making before accepting changes.
- I prioritize changes based on business value.
- This ensures controlled flexibility without impacting delivery commitments.

---

## How do you ensure smooth transition from development to support (Dev to Ops)?
- I implement DevOps practices to bridge development and operations.
- I ensure proper documentation, runbooks, and knowledge transfer sessions.
- I define SLAs, monitoring, and alerting mechanisms before go-live.
- I conduct readiness assessments including performance and security checks.
- I establish clear ownership across L1/L2/L3 support teams.
- This ensures seamless transition and stable operations post-deployment.

---

## How do you handle SLA breaches in service delivery?
- I immediately assess the severity and business impact of the breach.
- I initiate incident management protocols and communicate with stakeholders.
- I perform root cause analysis to identify underlying issues.
- I implement corrective and preventive actions to avoid recurrence.
- I track improvements through SLA metrics and reporting.
- I ensure transparency and accountability throughout the process.

---

## How do you manage production incidents in a high-availability system?
- I follow a structured incident management framework.
- I prioritize quick resolution to minimize downtime and business impact.
- I coordinate across teams to identify and resolve issues efficiently.
- I ensure clear communication with stakeholders during the incident.
- I conduct post-incident reviews and implement preventive measures.
- I strengthen monitoring and alerting systems to detect issues early.

---

## How do you ensure high availability and reliability of services?
- I design systems with redundancy and failover mechanisms.
- I implement proactive monitoring and automated alerting.
- I conduct regular resilience and disaster recovery testing.
- I ensure capacity planning to handle peak loads.
- I continuously improve system architecture based on performance insights.
- This ensures minimal downtime and consistent service reliability.

---

## How do you improve margins in a delivery portfolio?
- I optimize resource utilization and reduce inefficiencies.
- I implement automation to reduce manual effort and operational costs.
- I focus on high-value services and eliminate low-margin activities.
- I improve estimation accuracy to prevent cost overruns.
- I negotiate better vendor contracts where applicable.
- This leads to sustainable margin improvement.

---

## How do you handle financial risks in projects?
- I identify risks early during planning and estimation phases.
- I quantify risks in terms of cost and impact.
- I define mitigation strategies and contingency plans.
- I track financial performance regularly against budgets.
- I adjust plans proactively to avoid overruns.
- This ensures financial stability across projects.

## How do you manage vendor and third-party dependencies?
- I establish clear SLAs and performance expectations.
- I maintain regular communication and performance reviews.
- I track vendor deliverables against agreed timelines.
- I mitigate risks by having backup plans or alternate vendors.
- I ensure contractual compliance and accountability.
- This ensures smooth collaboration and minimal disruption.
