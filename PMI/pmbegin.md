## Introduction: Project Management

The polio vaccine, the Taj Mahal, the creation of the pocket calculator, the publication of a children's book...

These are the outcomes of projects where leaders applied project management practices, principles, processes, tools, and techniques to their work.

* Was the project completed in time?
* On budget?
* Was the project a success?

Imagine how the outcome could have been impacted if you were armed with the ever-evolving body of knowledge that has been sculpted over time by hundreds of project management industry professionals.
