# Certificate of the Business Continuity Institute

CBCI is a professional certification offered by the Business Continuity Institute (BCI) for individuals who understand the fundamentals of business continuity management (BCM).

## Purpose of CBCI

The certification validates knowledge in:

* Business Continuity Management (BCM)

* Risk assessment and impact analysis

* Incident response

* Disaster recovery planning

* Organizational resilience

It is based on the <u>Good Practice Guidelines (GPG)</u>, which is the global standard framework created by BCI.

Typical Topics Covered

CBCI certification covers the 6 Professional Practices of BCM:

Policy and Programme Management

Embedding BCM in the Organization

Analysis (Risk & Business Impact Analysis)

Design (BC strategies and solutions)

Implementation

Validation (Testing and exercising)

Certification Path

Common progression in BCI:

CBCI – Certificate level

AMBCI – Associate Member of BCI

MBCI – Member of BCI (advanced professional level)

Who Takes CBCI

IT disaster recovery professionals

Risk managers

Compliance professionals

Security professionals

Business continuity planners
