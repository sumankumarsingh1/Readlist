## Unit 1: Course Structure

### Course structure

The learning phase is split into 5 sessions plus a revision session with each broken down further into learning lessons and individual learning units. Although you will study at your own pace, we recommend setting study targets. Each section is designed to take around 5 – 8 hours and therefore a good target is to aim to complete 1 section per week.

The revision phase is designed to take around 10 hours of study and will prepare you for the **ACCA Recording Financial Transactions (FA1)** exam. We recommend you [book your On-Demand examination](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/student/exam-entry-and-administration/enter-an-exam.html) whilst in the learning phase to ensure you can take your exam at your preferred time and to set a target to work towards.

Learning support is provided when you need it within our course forums and discussion groups. Please do ask questions and as you progress through the course you can also help others who may be struggling with an area that you have mastered. We’ll explain how to use the forums in the next unit.

There are quizzes and practice tests throughout the course to ensure that you can apply your knowledge and to get you ready for your revision phase and the final exam. Note that the graded exam questions can only be attempted once whilst all other quizzes and tests may be attempted as many times as you wish. View the video below to learn more.

As the saying goes 'practice makes perfect', and there's no better way than to practice what you learn through each section by completing the optional exam questions and graded tests. We therefore encourage you to work through the optional questions first before attempting the graded tests. If you find that after attempting an optional test you didn't get the answer correct, you can learn
from the detailed feedback that is provided for each question.

* Don't be too disheartened upon getting a question incorrect. Learning is a journey and the key is to ensure that you learn from the mistakes you make. Consider revisiting the technical lesson that covered the concepts examined in that question. Often we find that when Learners revisit a lesson they understand things better the second time around.

* You can also reach out to us via the discussion forums or alternatively consider reading previous discussion forum threads. You might find that the question you seek clarification on may already have been answered. Once you are feeling properly
prepared for the technical content of a section, and have had sufficient practice with the optional questions you may also want to attempt the further optional questions. By attempting these you are better placed to score well on the graded tests.

* We have also included a revision section towards the end of the course. Here you'll have additional opportunities to attempt practice tests to really set you up for the exam. Our Data Insights reveal a strong positive correlation between a learner's performance in the graded exams and the marks that are obtained in the real exam. In other words attempting the optional exam questions and the graded tests can significantly increase your chances of passing the real exam.

* Additionally the repeated practice will help boost your confidence and reinforce the practical application of the technical content. It's a win-win situation.
