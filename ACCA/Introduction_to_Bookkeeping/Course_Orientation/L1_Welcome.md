### Tips for online study

Online learning is a new experience for many people and is obviously different from the traditional classroom environment. It can be intimidating, and it may feel overwhelming being responsible for your own schedule, so we want to share some simple tips for getting the most out of it. Click on each heading to learn more.

* **Think about your workplace**: One of the main challenges of studying online is finding a workspace that is free from distractions. Choose somewhere you feel at ease and don’t worry if you haven’t got access to a desk, just make sure you have enough space to sit comfortably with a laptop. Turn off your phone and ensure you have all your study tools close by so you don't have to keep getting up!

* **Set achievable goals**: You are far more likely to succeed if you have clear goals to work towards. It’s therefore important to understand what your learning objectives are. Try to identify which parts you'll find most difficult and consider how best to tackle them. Check your performance as you progress through your studies.

* **Create a study plan**: Set yourself a realistic schedule that fits around life and work. One of the advantages of online learning is the flexibility so make the most of it! You might find it helps to set yourself a deadline – an exam date, for example. Work backwards from this date and decide how much time to allocate to each task.

* **Find your learning style**: Some learning techniques are universal, such as asking questions, sharing knowledge and taking notes (tip: make sure to use the notes function on this course!) These are all active forms of learning that will solidify your knowledge of a subject. But we all learn in different ways and it’s important you test out different techniques that will help you get the most out of your studies.

* **Be part of your community**: Engaging with others on the forums is a fantastic way to improve your knowledge and boost motivation. Remember, sharing knowledge with your peers is one of the most effective forms of learning and gives you a chance to test your understanding of subjects.

* **Make use of the resources**: Ensure you make the most of the range of FREE resources at your disposal and build these into your study plans. There is more information about our resources in Lesson 3, Unit 2 of this orientation section.

* **Look after your wellbeing**: It’s important to look after your wellbeing. Feeling stressed and overworked is going to have a negative impact on your ability to learn so make sure to take regular breaks from the screen and don't take on too much at once. Reach out to your community when you need support and do check out the wellbeing hub on the [ACCA website](https://www.accaglobal.com/lk/en/student/wellbeing.html) for tips on balancing life, work and study.

* **Plan for exam success**: Lastly, book that on-demand exam! Having a date to work towards will give your learning focus and keep you motivated throughout your studies. You can find information about booking exams here.
