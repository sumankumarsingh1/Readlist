## Unit 1: Welcome to ACCA-X

**Welcome to the ACCA-X Introduction to Bookkeeping course, which is an introductory level course.**

This course will prepare you for the **Recording Financial Transactions (FA1)** exam building the knowledge and application skills needed for exam success.

This course is very interactive, and the learning activities have been designed to help develop your abilities as an accountant in the real world.

We hope you’ll enjoy taking the course, but we know that learning online can be challenging, so we have a dedicated ACCA-X team to support you throughout your learning journey.

In this orientation section we will explain the course structure and features, and help you get the most out of this course.

But first here’s an ACCA member explaining the benefits of being a professional accountant and an ACCA member.
